A Mission That Begins With Kindness.

Helper Hands was started by Nikhil with one simple dream: to bring happiness, support and hope to people who need it most.

We believe that every child deserves nutritious food, educational opportunities and the happiness of a childhood filled with care.

Our initiative focuses on small but meaningful actions, including food distribution, educational support, gifts, clothing and essential supplies.

We are starting small, but our intentions are big. We believe real change begins when ordinary people come together to help others.

We don't believe in making big promises. We believe in taking real action, documenting our work and maintaining transparency.

Our Mission

Helping underprivileged children and needy families through food, gifts, clothes, educational materials and essential supplies.
